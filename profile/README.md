# Intelligent Interactive Systems Group at Harvard
The Intelligent Interactive Systems Group at Harvard was founded in September of 2009. We design, build and evaluate interactive systems that have some machine intelligence under the hood. This work requires simultaneous innovation in design and computation so we engage a wide range of methods from qualitative research, through design and quantitative controlled experiments, to building new algorithms and implementing working systems.

Some of the things we are are currently working on include:
- [human-AI interaction design principles for AI-assisted decision-making](https://iis.seas.harvard.edu/projects/#decision-making)
- [decision support tools that support contestation and optimize for the welfare of decision subjects](https://iis.seas.harvard.edu/projects/#contestation)
- [tools for detecting and quantifying motor impairments](https://iis.seas.harvard.edu/projects/#hevelius)
- [tools to support humanitarian frontline negotiators](https://iis.seas.harvard.edu/projects/#negotiation)
- [understanding how to structure collaborations between community organizations and AI technologists such that these collaborations bring real benefits to communities](https://iis.seas.harvard.edu/projects/#ai4sg)
- and we continue to [work](https://iis.seas.harvard.edu/projects/#labinthewild) on [LabintheWild](https://www.labinthewild.org/)!
